## Design Context

### Users
Public-facing skywatchers using a phone outdoors during the Artemis II launch window. They need a fast way to understand whether the rocket is likely visible from where they are and where to aim their eyes immediately.

### Brand Personality
Field-ready, mission-focused, and lucid. The interface should feel calm under pressure, legible in bright conditions, and grounded in real launch-day utility rather than sci-fi decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
Outdoor-first light theme with strong contrast, warm launch-inspired accents, and a purposeful instrument-panel feel. The app should feel like a compact field guide, not a dashboard template or a map-heavy logistics tool.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize fast orientation over dense information.
2. Keep critical launch guidance readable in daylight at arm’s length.
3. Surface uncertainty honestly when visibility or trajectory assumptions are approximate.
4. Preserve a distinct mission-event feel without compromising utility.
